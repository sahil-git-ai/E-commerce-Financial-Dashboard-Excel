# E-commerce-Financial-Dashboard-Excel
📌 Project Overview
This Excel dashboard analyzes e-commerce sales data of 5,000 orders across 3 product categories — Fashion, Electronics, and Accessories — spanning 8 major Indian cities. The goal was to track sales performance, payment trends, profit vs discount impact, and monthly revenue patterns.

📊 Key Metrics Tracked
Metric                 Value
Total Sales            ₹90,17,88,978 (~$90M+)
Total Profit           ₹16,02,81,633
Total Orders            5,000
Average Sales Value    ₹1,80,357
Average Profit Value   ₹32,056

🔍 Key Insights from the Dashboard

Top Category: Fashion leads in both sales and profit — highest bar in category-wise sales chart, making it the primary revenue driver
Top City: Bangalore generates the highest sales among all 8 cities (670+ average), followed by Mumbai and Pune
Top Products: Headphones and Laptop are the top 2 selling products — Camera and Monitor lag behind significantly
Payment Mode: UPI and Card are the most used payment methods — COD still exists but lower, suggesting digital payment adoption is high
Profit vs Discount: Electronics shows highest discount given but also highest profit — Fashion has lower discount but strong margins
Monthly Trend: Sales dip significantly between July–October — suggests seasonal slowdown, opportunity for promotional campaigns


🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used

Tool: Microsoft Excel
Visuals Used: Donut chart, bar chart, 3D column chart, line chart, pie chart
Techniques: Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers for city-wise filtering
Data Cleaning: Removed duplicates, standardized category names, formatted currency columns


💼 Business Use Case
This dashboard is built for e-commerce business owners and category managers to:

Identify top-performing products and cities for targeted marketing
Understand payment mode preferences to optimize checkout experience
Track monthly sales dips for campaign planning
Analyze profit vs discount trade-off by category
